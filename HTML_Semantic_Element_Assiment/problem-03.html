Important Healthcare Terms

Consultation
A discussion with a healthcare professional regarding a patient's concern.
Follow-up
A later appointment to review progress or continue care.
OPD
Outpatient Department where patients receive consultation without hospital admission.
Appointment
A scheduled time for meeting a healthcare professional.
