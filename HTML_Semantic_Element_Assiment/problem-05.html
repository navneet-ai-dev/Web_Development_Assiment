Event Overview

The National Career & Education Fair 2026 is a platform where students and job seekers can explore education, internship, placement, scholarship and apprenticeship opportunities.

The event aims to connect participants with universities, companies, training organizations and career experts.

Registration is required for participants who want to attend the fair and its scheduled sessions.

Objectives of the Event

  • Help students explore career opportunities.
  • Provide information about higher education.
  • Connect participants with organizations.
  • Provide internship and placement information.
  • Share scholarship and apprenticeship opportunities.

Preparation Guide

Prepare Before Visiting

Participants should prepare their academic information and career interests before attending the event.

Bring a valid identification document and keep an updated resume if you are looking for internships or placements.

"The right opportunity begins with the right preparation."

Recommended Preparation

  1. Research participating organizations.
  2. Prepare your resume.
  3. Identify your preferred career sector.
  4. Prepare questions for representatives.
  5. Attend relevant information sessions.
  6. Follow up after the event.

Preparation Tips

  • Arrive before your selected session.
  • Dress appropriately for professional interactions.
  • Keep important documents ready.
  • Note down useful contact information.

Important Terms

Career Fair
An event where participants explore career and employment opportunities.
Internship
A temporary learning opportunity that provides practical experience.
Placement
An opportunity to join an organization for employment after selection.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students for education.
Apprenticeship
A structured training opportunity combining practical work and learning.

A participant may explore opportunities from undergraduate level to postgraduate level and beyond.
