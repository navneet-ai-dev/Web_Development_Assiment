Hiring Process

  1. Submit your application.
  2. Resume screening.
  3. Technical assessment.
  4. Technical interview.
  5. HR interview.
  6. Receive the final offer.

Hiring Terms

Screening
Initial review of the candidate's application and resume.
Technical Round
Assessment of technical knowledge and problem-solving skills.
HR Round
Discussion about communication, goals and workplace suitability.
Offer
Final employment offer provided to the selected candidate.

Recruiter Tips

Keep your resume clear, updated and relevant to the position you are applying for.

Mention your important technical skills and projects.
