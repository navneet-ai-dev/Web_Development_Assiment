Registration Process

  1. Choose the event in which you want to participate.
  2. Read the eligibility and event rules carefully.
  3. Complete the online registration form.
  4. Provide correct student and contact information.
  5. Pay the registration fee if applicable.
  6. Submit the registration form before the deadline.
  7. Save your registration/reference number.
  8. Report to the venue according to the event schedule.

Mandatory: Participants must carry a valid college identity card during the fest.

Important Fest Terms

Open Event
An event in which eligible participants from different groups can participate.
Team Event
An event where two or more participants compete together as a team.
Individual Event
An event where a single participant competes independently.
Registration
The process of officially enrolling for a fest event.
Coordinator
A person responsible for managing and organizing a particular event.
Final Round
The last stage of a competition where finalists compete for the winning position.

Fest Rules

  • Participants must carry their valid college ID card.
  • Participants must report at the venue before the scheduled time.
  • Registration must be completed before the announced deadline.
  • Participants must follow instructions given by event coordinators.
  • Any form of unfair practice may result in disqualification.
  • Participants must maintain discipline and cleanliness.
  • Organizers reserve the right to modify the schedule if required.
  • Decisions of the judging panel will be considered final.
