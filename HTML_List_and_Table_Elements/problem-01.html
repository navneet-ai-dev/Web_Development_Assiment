Admission Overview

Eligibility

Students must satisfy the academic requirements of their selected program before submitting the application.

  • Undergraduate: Passed Class 12 or equivalent.
  • Postgraduate: Completed a recognized bachelor's degree.
  • Required subjects must be studied according to the selected course.
  • Applicants must provide valid academic documents.
  • Entrance requirements may apply to selected programs.

Application Process

Follow these steps in the given order:

  1. Visit the official university admission website.
  2. Register using your email address and mobile number.
  3. Fill in the personal and academic information.
  4. Select your preferred program.
  5. Upload the required documents.
  6. Review all information carefully.
  7. Pay the applicable application fee.
  8. Submit the application form.
  9. Download and save the application confirmation.

Important: Check all details before final submission.

Visit Official Admission Website

Required Documents

  • Class 10 marksheet/certificate
  • Class 12 marksheet/certificate
  • Recent passport-size photograph
  • Valid identity proof
  • Transfer certificate, if applicable
  • Migration certificate, if applicable
  • Category certificate, if applicable
  • Entrance examination scorecard, if required

Important Admission Terms

Eligibility
The academic conditions a student must satisfy for admission.
Merit List
A list of applicants arranged according to their qualifying performance.
Counselling
The process through which eligible students select or are allotted programs.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students according to specified criteria.
Registration
The process of entering applicant information into the university admission system.
