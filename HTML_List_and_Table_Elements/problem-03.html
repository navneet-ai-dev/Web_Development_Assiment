Steps Before a Practical Class

  1. Read the practical topic before entering the laboratory.
  2. Bring the required notebook and study material.
  3. Check the assigned computer or laboratory equipment.
  4. Listen to the instructor's instructions carefully.
  5. Complete the practical task and record the result.
  6. Submit the practical work before leaving the laboratory.

Academic Rules

  • Students must maintain regular attendance.
  • Students must carry their identity card.
  • Students should reach the classroom before the lecture begins.
  • Mobile phones should be used only when permitted by the faculty.
  • Laboratory equipment must be handled carefully.
  • Assignments must be submitted before the announced deadline.
  • Students must maintain discipline inside the department.

Academic Terms

Lecture
A teaching session focused mainly on explaining theoretical concepts.
Lab
A practical session where students perform experiments or programming tasks.
Tutorial
A guided academic session used for practice and problem solving.
Internal Assessment
Evaluation conducted during the semester through tests, assignments or activities.
Practical
A hands-on academic activity designed to apply theoretical knowledge.
