Company Overview

About TCS ™

Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) is a global technology and consulting organization. TCS focuses on innovation, digital transformation, artificial intelligence and long-term customer relationships.

TCS promotes continuous learning, collaboration and professional growth. Its India careers program provides opportunities for entry-level candidates, interns and experienced professionals.
