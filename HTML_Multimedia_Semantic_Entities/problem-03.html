Destination Overview

Explore Dubai

Dubai is one of the world's most popular tourist destinations. It is famous for modern architecture, shopping, beaches, desert experiences and cultural attractions.

Visitors can enjoy luxury experiences, adventure activities and cultural exploration throughout the year.

The city combines traditional Arabian culture with modern technology and world-class infrastructure.

Dubai is approximately one of the most visited cities in the Middle East.

Popular Attractions

Burj Khalifa

Visitors can enjoy panoramic views of Dubai from the observation areas of this famous skyscraper.

Dubai Desert Safari

Desert experiences can include dune drives, traditional entertainment and opportunities to experience the desert environment.

Dubai Marina

Dubai Marina is known for its waterfront views, restaurants, walking areas and modern architecture.
