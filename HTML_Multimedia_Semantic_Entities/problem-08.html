Welcome to Smart Finance

Smart Finance is an educational platform that helps learners understand banking, savings, cards, loans and responsible personal-finance practices.

Remember: Save & Plan = Financial Security.

Types of Bank Accounts

Savings Account

A savings account is useful for keeping money safely while earning interest on the deposited amount.

Current Account

A current account is generally designed for frequent transactions and business activities.

Credit & Debit Cards

Debit Card

A debit card allows you to make payments using money available in your bank account.

Credit Card

A credit card allows eligible users to borrow money up to an approved limit and repay it according to the terms.

Understanding Loans

Personal Loan

A personal loan can be used for eligible personal expenses and is generally repaid through scheduled installments.

Education Loan

Education loans may help eligible students meet approved education-related expenses.

Financial Tips

  • Create a monthly budget.
  • Track your income and expenses.
  • Save a part of your income regularly.
  • Avoid unnecessary spending.
  • Understand loan terms before borrowing.
  • Keep important financial information secure.
  • Compare charges and benefits before choosing a product.

Steps to Open a Bank Account

  1. Select a suitable bank account.
  2. Check eligibility and required documents.
  3. Complete the application form.
  4. Submit the required information.
  5. Complete the verification process.
  6. Receive your account details.

Important Finance Terms

Interest Rate
The percentage used to calculate interest on a financial amount.
EMI
Equated Monthly Instalment paid towards certain loans.
Principal
The original amount of money borrowed or invested.
Tenure
The period over which a financial product or loan is maintained.
