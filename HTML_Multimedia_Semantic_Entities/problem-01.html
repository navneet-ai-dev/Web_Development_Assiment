Welcome to GLA University

GLA University is a multidisciplinary university located in Mathura, Uttar Pradesh. The university offers undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programs.

GLA provides students with opportunities in education, research, innovation and professional development.

Admission Announcement

GLA University offers various programs in engineering, computer science, management, sciences and other disciplines.

Students should check the official admission information before applying.

Important: Admission requirements may vary according to the selected program.
