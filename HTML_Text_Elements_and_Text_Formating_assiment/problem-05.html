Science Notes

Chemistry

Water is represented by the chemical formula H2O.

Carbon dioxide is represented as CO2.

Oxygen molecule is represented as O2.

Sulfuric acid is represented as H2SO4.

Ammonia is represented as NH3.

Atoms and molecules are important concepts in chemistry.

Physics

Area is measured in square metres, written as m2.

Volume is commonly measured in cubic metres, written as m3.

The speed of light is approximately 3 × 108 m/s.

Force is an important concept in physics and is measured in newtons.

Biology

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants prepare their food using sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide.

The chemical formula of glucose is C6H12O6.

DNA carries genetic information in living organisms.

The human body contains many different types of cells that perform different functions.

Carbon dioxide = CO2
