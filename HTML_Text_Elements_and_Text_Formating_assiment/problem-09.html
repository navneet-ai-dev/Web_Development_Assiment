Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

1. Students must maintain proper discipline and respectful behaviour inside the college campus.

2. Students must carry their valid college identity card whenever they are on the campus.

Attendance

3. Students should attend their classes regularly and maintain the required attendance.

Students are expected to be punctual for their classes. Regular attendance helps students understand the subjects better.

Examination

4. Students must follow all examination instructions given by the college and examination authorities.

Students must reach the examination room on time.
Late entry may be restricted according to examination rules.

Assignment Submission

5. Students must complete and submit their assignments within the given deadline.

Assignment Deadline: 5 September 2026

Assignments should be properly written, complete, and submitted according to the instructions provided by the teacher.

Discipline

6. Students must maintain discipline and should not disturb teachers or other students during academic activities.

Any serious violation of college rules may result in appropriate disciplinary action. Students are expected to behave responsibly.

Important Notice

Important: All students must follow the college rules and complete their academic work on time.

Students should regularly check official announcements for changes in academic schedules, deadlines, and other important information.
