GLA University - Greater Noida Campus

GLA University Greater Noida is an industry-integrated learning campus located at 15A, Knowledge Park II, Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh. The campus focuses on quality education, practical learning and industry exposure.

About

GLA University Greater Noida provides an industry-centric learning environment with experienced faculty, professional mentorship, practical projects and opportunities for students to develop technical and professional skills.

Programs

The Greater Noida campus offers programs including B.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering, B.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering - Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, B.Tech. Electronics & Computer Engineering, AI-Enabled BBA, AI-Enabled MBA (Hons.) and MCA in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning.

Admissions are open for the 2026 batch.

Campus Life

Campus life at GLA University Greater Noida includes cultural activities, student events, music sessions and sports such as cricket, chess, carrom, athletics, table tennis and volleyball.

Admission

The admission process includes online registration, payment of the registration fee, GLA Entrance Test (GLAET), checking the result and completing the further admission process.

Important: Students should complete their admission process through the official GLA University admission portal.
