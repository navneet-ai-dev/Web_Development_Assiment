Breaking News: College Launches New Innovation and Technology Center

New facility aims to give students more opportunities for practical learning

A new Innovation and Technology Center has been inaugurated at the college to support student projects, technology experiments and practical learning. The center is designed to provide students with a dedicated place where they can work on innovative ideas and develop technical skills.

The new facility was inaugurated on 6 September 2026 on the college campus. Students, faculty members and college representatives attended the inauguration ceremony. The event included presentations about the purpose of the center and its future activities.

The center will provide students with opportunities to work on different academic and technology-based projects. Students can use the facility for project development, demonstrations and collaborative learning. The college plans to encourage students to participate in practical activities alongside their regular studies.

According to the college media club, the main purpose of the new center is to connect classroom learning with practical experience. This initiative is expected to encourage students to explore new ideas and develop problem-solving skills. More workshops and student activities are planned for the future.
