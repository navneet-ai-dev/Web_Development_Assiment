TechFest 2026

Technology, Innovation and Ideas for the Future

Introduction

TechFest 2026 is a technology and innovation event organized for students who are interested in modern technology, programming, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and innovative ideas.

The event provides students with an opportunity to learn new technologies, explore creative ideas and participate in technology-based activities.

Highlights

TechFest 2026 will include student projects, technology demonstrations, programming activities and interactive learning sessions.

Students can explore practical applications of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning and understand how technology can be used to solve real-world problems.

Date and Venue

Date:
20 October 2026

Venue:
GLA University, Greater Noida Campus
Knowledge Park II, Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh

Registration Deadline: 10 October 2026

Speakers

TechFest 2026 will feature sessions by technology professionals, educators and industry experts. Speakers will discuss Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, programming, innovation and the future of technology.

Students will have the opportunity to learn from practical experiences and gain knowledge about the use of technology in real-world projects.
